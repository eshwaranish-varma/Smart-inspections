2. How the AI Grounds the Observation

This is the most important explanation for clients: how the project proves that the generated observation is connected back to real evidence and real regulations.

Source Evidence Grounding

The generated observation is not accepted only because it sounds correct. The system verifies whether the evidence statements actually appear in, or strongly match, the original inspection notes and OCR text.

CFR Citation Grounding

The generated citation is checked against regulatory reference data. This tells the reviewer whether the cited regulation is likely a valid and relevant regulatory anchor.

IOM and Regulatory Reference Support

The platform also retrieves reference material from the regulatory knowledge base so the narrative is informed by inspection practice and compliance context, not only by the raw notes.

Client-Friendly Technical Keywords

  • Canonical source text: the trusted raw note and OCR body
  • Chunk retrieval: breaking large text into searchable evidence blocks
  • Embedding similarity: ranking relevant evidence by semantic closeness
  • Grounding: proving the narrative is supported by source evidence
  • Citation matching: validating proposed CFR references
  • Explainability: showing the reviewer why the system produced this result

Practical meaning: a well-written observation is not enough. The project aims to show that the observation is supported by evidence, aligned to regulation, and auditable before the business relies on it.

3. Step-by-Step Metric Calculation Flow

This flow explains how the platform turns a drafted observation into measurable quality signals.

Evidence Precision

Measures how many evidence bullets are actually supported by the source text.

  • Higher precision means fewer unsupported claims
  • Low precision signals possible hallucination risk

Grounding Recall

Measures whether the observation has at least one clearly grounded evidence anchor.

  • High recall means the observation is anchored
  • Low recall means evidence support is weak

Grounding F1

Combines precision and recall into one balanced grounding score.

  • Used as a core evidence reliability metric
  • Feeds confidence and dashboard scoring

Narrative Grounding Ratio

Measures how much of the final narrative overlaps with terms from the source text.

  • Acts as an anti-hallucination signal
  • Useful even when wording is more polished

Confidence and Status

Blends grounding, citation quality, structure, and retrieval quality into a reviewer-friendly score.

  • Produces pass, warning, or failed status
  • Supports reviewer prioritization

4. Minimal Technical Output Model

After scoring is complete, the project exposes three clear outputs for reviewers and clients.

Observation Quality View

Shows whether the generated observation is structurally complete and sufficiently grounded.

  • Structure quality
  • Grounding score
  • Citation validity
  • Confidence level

Explainability View

Shows the technical path used to support the observation.

  • Retrieved evidence chunks
  • Evidence linkage
  • CFR candidate matching
  • Traceable reasoning path

Business Review View

Shows whether the observation can be used confidently for remediation and decision-making.

  • Inspection-level quality summary
  • Risk and hallucination flags
  • Reviewer escalation signals
  • Readiness for approval or revision

Business meaning: the final narrative affects prevention plans, quality-system improvements, training actions, and cost-effective remediation strategies. That is why the platform measures grounding before the narrative is trusted.

After AI generates inspection observations, the platform validates them through evidence grounding, regulatory citation alignment, reference-context retrieval, and objective metric calculation. The result is a reviewable, explainable, and business-ready observation pipeline rather than a simple text-generation tool.
