Smart Inspections: AI-Assisted FDA 483 & EIR Drafting

OCR, regulatory RAG, and human-in-the-loop workflows for consistent inspection documentation.

Authors: Capstone team — Smart Inspection (George Mason University) Repository: github.com/sathyapalreddypeddakkagari/SmartInspectionTeam

Problem Space

Why This Matters at AI Day

Public-sector inspection teams produce high-stakes structured documents where errors and inconsistency carry regulatory and safety implications. This project connects AI literacy (how LLM outputs are produced and limited), governance (audit trails, review gates, citation verification), and workforce readiness (supervisor review, signatures, training-oriented UI)—themes central to responsible deployment of generative AI.

Core challenge: FDA Form 483 observations and Establishment Inspection Reports are labor-intensive, easy to make inconsistent across investigators, and difficult to trace when multiple people touch the same case.

Method note

The backend combines LangChain with Gemini or OpenAI (per LLM_PROVIDER), optional FAISS + embeddings for knowledge retrieval with keyword fallback, and CitationService to map model CFR strings to canonical sections—plus optional grounding metrics for dashboards.

Contributions

  • Novel Dataset: Title 21 section table plus FY2025 inspection observation spreadsheet and IOM PDFs under data/ (per repository README).
  • Open-Source Tool: Next.js workflow GUI, FastAPI AI/OCR service, Docker Compose + NGINX path-based routing, PostgreSQL/Supabase-friendly schema.
  • Policy Framework: Supervisor review, electronic signatures, document versioning, pipeline run logging, and explicit CFR review flags when matches are weak.

AI Day alignment

Demonstrates transparent AI assistance in a regulated-document setting: investigators see retrieval context, citation resolution scores, and structured review states—supporting literacy and accountable use rather than “black box” automation.
